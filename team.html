our story

Founded by people who wished, growing up, that someone had simply asked.

Nayrah began as a quiet conversation between two friends — Anupriya and Priya — about the kind of mental health support we wished we'd had access to in our twenties. Honest, unhurried, free of jargon, and built around the person sitting across the room rather than a worksheet.

We started small on purpose. A small practice means we can pay attention to the things that matter — the pace each person needs, the texture of a hard week, the quiet moments where something finally clicks. As we grow, that's the standard we keep.

Every therapist who joins Nayrah goes through a careful match — clinically, but also for the way they hold a room. We'd rather take longer to add the right person than fill a slot. That's the promise behind the practice.
